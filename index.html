Questions people ask

Is the agent a person?

No. Your tax agent is software. It fills in your return from HMRC and your connected accounts, explains the figures and files once you approve. You check and approve everything before it is filed.

How is it free?

This is a design concept. A real service would be paid for by optional extras, never by a cut of your refund.

What can the agent do with HMRC?

See your tax record and file your Self Assessment return, and only after you approve it. It will not change your bank details, and any refund is paid by HMRC straight to you. You can remove its access at any time in your HMRC account.

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes of questions and about 5 minutes to authorise us with HMRC. Once your accounts are connected, your return is usually ready to check within minutes.

What if I owe tax?

Your agent shows you the amount and how it was worked out before anything is filed. You pay HMRC directly. We never handle your tax payments.

Who is this not for yet?

Directors of limited companies. Their returns need extra company details that we do not cover yet.
