Questions people ask

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes of questions and 5 minutes to authorise your accountant with HMRC. Our target is a return ready for you to approve within 3 working days of your documents arriving.

What do I need to have ready?

Your Unique Taxpayer Reference (the 10-digit number on HMRC letters) and your National Insurance number. No payslips or P60: those figures come from HMRC.

What can my accountant do with HMRC?

See your tax record and file your Self Assessment return, and only after you approve it. They will not change your bank details, and any refund is paid by HMRC straight to you. You can remove them at any time.

What if I owe tax?

Your accountant shows you the amount and how it was worked out before anything is filed. You pay HMRC directly. We never handle your tax payments.

Who is this not for yet?

Directors of limited companies. Their returns need extra company details that we do not cover yet.
