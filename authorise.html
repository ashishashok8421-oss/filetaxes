Authorise Priya with HMRC

This lets Priya see your tax record and file your return for you. About 5 minutes.

Needed
1

Your tax details

HMRC uses these to find your record.

10 digits. On letters from HMRC about Self Assessment.
On a payslip or in the HMRC app.
2

What you are agreeing to

Priya can
  • See your pay, tax paid and Self Assessment record
  • File this return, after you approve it
  • Speak to HMRC about your return
Priya cannot
  • File anything without your approval
  • Change your bank details with HMRC
  • Receive your refund. HMRC pays you.
3

Approve it with HMRC

We take you to HMRC. Sign in with your Government Gateway user ID, the login you use for HMRC online, and approve the request. Then you come straight back here.

No Government Gateway login? Ask HMRC for an authorisation code by post

Priya is authorised

HMRC has confirmed the request. Your pay and tax figures are on their way to Priya.
